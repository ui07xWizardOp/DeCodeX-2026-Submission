VOLTRIDE STRATEGY: POCKET GUIDE

Executive Cheat Sheet

Prepared By: Priyobrata Chatterjee, student at KIIT UNIVERSITY Roll NO.:23052904

🎯 THE 10-SECOND SUMMARY

VoltRide cancels 30% of rides because it dispatches dying cars, ignores its best drivers, and incentivizes charging during rush hour. We are fighting our own system.

📊 THE NUMBERS

Metric Current Target Gap
Cancellation Rate 29.6% <15% -14.6%
Monthly Failed Rides 739 <375 -364
Revenue Loss ~$18K ~$9K ~$9K/month

🔥 TOP 3 FIXES

1. STOP DISPATCHING DYING CARS ⚡

The Fix: Block dispatch if battery <25%.
Why: 87.5% of these rides fail. It's a waste of time.
Cost: $0.
Status: 🟢 DO IT NOW

2. FEED THE "GOLDILOCKS" DRIVERS 💎

The Fix: Prioritize drivers with 30-60% battery.
Why: They are our most reliable group (lowest cancellations).
Cost: Minimal (Algorithm tune).
Status: 🟡 TEST NEXT WEEK

3. FLIP THE CHARGING SCHEDULE ⏰

The Fix: Pay drivers to charge at 2 PM, not 6 PM.
Why: We run out of cars during rush hour because they're all plugging in.
Cost: Incentive budget.
Status: 🟡 PLAN FOR NEXT MONTH

💡 MYTH BUSTERS

  • ❌ Myth: "Range anxiety kicks in at 50%."
    • ✅ Truth: Drivers are SAFEST at 30-60%. They are hungry for work.
  • ❌ Myth: "We need more chargers."
    • ✅ Truth: More chargers near hotspots actually INCREASE cancellations (drivers camp there).
  • ❌ Myth: "Weather is killing us."
    • ✅ Truth: Rain adds 1.7% to cancellations. Bad dispatch logic adds 10%.

3.  **Sample Size Fragility:**
    - The risk mapping (Mumbai 10AM) is based on N=6.
    - *Critique:* While acknowledged, the visual confidence might mislead a fast reader. The *Quick Reference* should perhaps add an asterisk or "Low Confidence" marker to specific time-window claims.

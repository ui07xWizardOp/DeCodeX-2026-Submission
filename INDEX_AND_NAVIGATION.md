## 🔍 KEY FINDINGS QUICK REFERENCE

### Critical Data Quality Issues Found
1. **Ghost Cancellation Error:** 40% data loss (290 cancellations missed)
2. **Statistical Fragility:** Many risk windows have N<10 samples
3. **Range Anxiety Myth:** Data contradicts conventional wisdom
4. **Infrastructure Paradox:** Proximity to chargers increases cancellations

### Top 5 Validated Insights
1. **The 20% Kill Zone:** 87.5% failure rate (vs 25% baseline)
2. **The Compliant Middle:** 30-60% battery = lowest cancellations
3. **Rich Car Cherry-Picking:** >80% battery = higher cancellations
4. **The Timing Trap:** Drivers charge during peak demand
5. **Ghost Cancellations:** Proper baseline is 29.6% (not 18-20%)

### Top 3 Recommendations
1. **25% Battery Floor:** $0 cost, 8-10% improvement, 1 week
2. **Smart Dispatch:** $10K cost, 3-5% improvement, 4 weeks
3. **Off-Peak Incentives:** $30K cost, 5-8% improvement, 8 weeks
